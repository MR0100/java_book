# L5 — Architecture & Engineering Leadership

Design systems at scale and lead the people who build them. As much about judgment and communication as technology.

> [!NOTE]
> **Tier:** Lead / Staff  
> **Prerequisites:** [L4 module](../L4-backend-engineering/)

## What you'll learn

- Design principles, DDD, and architectural styles.
- Distributed systems and a repeatable system-design methodology.
- Worked system designs end to end.
- The leadership craft: ADRs, mentoring, strategy, incidents.
